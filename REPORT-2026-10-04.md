# prompten.xyz — engagement report, 28 September – 4 October 2026

Written for someone picking this up later who wasn't here, like
REPORT-2026-08-10.md before it. It assumes you know the stack (Next.js 14 App
Router, Supabase, Vercel) and nothing about what happened.

**Read alongside:** `SECURITY-NOTES.md` (live security posture),
`VERIFICATION-NOTES.md` (how to check things so the check means something),
`ERASURE-PROCEDURE.md` and `CERTIFICATE-CLAIMS.md` (procedures). Every change
below is on `main`; the commit hashes are there to read, not to trust.

---

## 0. The one-paragraph version

A third-party audit of the live site was worked through in seven groups,
each verified on a preview and then on production before the next began.
Three things grew out of it. First, the certificate rules were made single
and true: one definition of "complete", and a grandfather clause that turned
out to cover 0 of 1,668 accounts was removed. Second, course reviews were
built and switched on, moderated before publication, with the admin
password replaced by a listed signed-in account. Third, the `course_events`
analytics table, whose names and emails outlived account deletions against
the privacy policy's word, was retired and dropped. The site makes no request
to Google for fonts, unknown paths return 404, and the privacy policy and
terms describe what the database actually holds.

## 1. What changed, by area

### Content
- **Lesson 1 accuracy** (`c4e37e3`): four claims corrected; the long-context
  guidance is now cited and scoped to 20,000+ tokens; quiz 0-0 questions 2
  and 3 rewritten.
- **Lesson times are derived, not typed** (`c4e37e3`, `6541a66`): `dur` is
  computed from word count (200 wpm) plus 45 s per quiz question, labelled
  "N min read". The integrity guard fails the build if a stored value drifts
  from the formula. Before: 501 minutes claimed against about 84 of reading.
- **The finale moved** from lesson 6-2 to 7-3, the real last lesson (`986a34a`).
- **Brand** (`256337a`): the last live "PromptMastery" string, the diagram
  caption on all 26 lessons, is gone. The first check that "confirmed" it
  gone was case-sensitive, and that is how it survived
  (VERIFICATION-NOTES.md, searches).

### Routing and SEO
- Unknown single-segment paths return 404, not a 200 copy of the landing
  page (`16c0925`). `/quiz` 301s to `/course`.
- The apex domain redirected with a 307 from a Vercel domain setting, while
  `vercel.json` claimed a 301 that never fired. Now 308 in the domain
  setting, and the dead rule deleted (`43a9ebe`).
- Sitemap: 7 URLs, matching the indexable set exactly.

### Accessibility, mobile, landing
- Every control that navigates is a real link; one `<h1>` per page; visible
  focus (`c38bdee`).
- Lesson diagrams open full screen on tap, rotate on a portrait phone, and are
  keyboard-reachable with Escape to close; hero stats are a 2×2 grid at phone
  width (`9340ac3`).
- Positioning as a progression ("From no AI background to production-level
  prompting"); line icons in place of emoji; "Made by BMS" links to /about;
  the real certificate component renders as a SAMPLE on the homepage
  (`1ea5ab8`).
- **Fonts are self-hosted** via `next/font` (`e42bf72`). A full network
  capture across the homepage, a public lesson, /auth and /cert showed no
  request to fonts.googleapis.com or fonts.gstatic.com.

### Certificates
- **One definition of complete**: `isCourseComplete()`, used by the issue
  route and the client gate alike (`00009c5`). The client gate had disagreed
  with the server.
- **The grandfather clause protected no one** (`9e8bae4`, `23373da`): it let
  accounts created before the 2026-04-20 syllabus expansion qualify at 22
  lessons. Measured: 0 of 1,668 accounts predate the cutoff, because the
  pre-Supabase cohort was re-created at migration time with later
  timestamps. Removed; old-cohort claims are handled by hand
  (CERTIFICATE-CLAIMS.md), and the guard fails if a date-keyed exception
  reappears. Admin stats followed (migrations 012, 013).
- The certificate page renders the name **as issued** (`cert.name`), not the
  live profile name (`e3064cd`). A certificate is a record.

### Course reviews (live since 1 October 2026)
Built in seven steps, each verified before the next:
- **Table** (014): one review per person, text only, cascades with its
  author, service role only. Verified by attempting every forbidden read and
  write as `anon` and `authenticated` (all refused, 42501) against a control
  that succeeded.
- **Legal text first** (`66b3a95`): privacy §3 "Reviews you choose to
  publish", the consent basis, retention, and a terms licence that is
  non-exclusive and display-only, and says "We won't change your words".
  `consent_version` on each review records the policy date shown.
- **Write path** (`9ce5f60`): identity from the verified token; eligibility
  from `certificates`; display name derived server-side ("First L." or full);
  every write is `pending`. Forty tamper assertions passed, with a control
  that failed when the pending rule was removed.
- **Invitation** on the certificate and profile pages (`53d85d7`). Save is
  never disabled for validation: a disabled button isn't focusable.
- **Admin is a listed account** (`7aa699c`, `8d79492`): `ADMIN_USER_IDS`
  replaced the shared password. The owner verified the new path on
  production before the old one was removed, and `ADMIN_SECRET` was then
  deleted from all three Vercel environments. Moderation: approve, reject,
  unpublish, delete; no edit action exists.
- **Homepage section** (`54c8bb6`, `6b8bdb5`): static, with
  `revalidatePath('/')` on every change to the approved set and an hourly
  backstop. 0 reviews: no section. 1–3: a grid. 4+: a scrolling row with no
  library and no auto-advance. Any fetch failure renders the page without
  the section. A hang is cut at 4 s, verified against a socket that accepts
  and never answers: 4.4 s, against a still-hanging control at 15 s. A
  throwaway build with the flag on and no database completed and rendered
  the page without the section.
- **Switched on** (`b1fff12`); canary run by the owner end to end; the
  owner's own review deleted afterwards, because a review from the site's
  owner on its own homepage is what the no-anonymous rule exists to prevent.
- **First reviews published, observed on production.** On 4 October at
  10:38 UTC the owner approved two reviews from `/admin`, from two different
  graduates. One was submitted on 3 October at 12:38 UTC, the other at 21:22
  UTC that evening, after that day's check. Both decisions are recorded
  against the owner's account. The owner saw them live on the homepage. The
  served homepage HTML has the "What graduates say" section with 2 cards and
  no user ids or consent fields. One timing detail: the first plain request
  for `/` that I made after the approval returned the page *without* the
  section, and requests seconds later had it (cache HIT, age 20–29 s). The
  first request after a rebuild can still be served the old page.

### `course_events` retired (3–4 October 2026)
The table held name, email and a timestamp per registration and completion.
It had no foreign key to accounts, so every account deletion left these
behind, and the policy promised a 24-month expiry that nothing enforced.
Nothing had read it since August.
1. `/api/track` and both callers removed (`6b02b77`, live 15:31:49 UTC).
2. Privacy policy: usage events removed from §2, §4 and §7, plus a sentence
   saying they were kept until 4 October 2026 and deleted from the database
   (`0b3a458`, `fe761dc`).
3. Erasure procedure: the manual step is gone (`9942602`).
4. **Migration 015** dropped the table at 2026-10-04 08:13:09 UTC: **1,136
   rows**. It aborts unless the count matches the owner-confirmed figure and
   no row post-dates the removal. It ran only after an account had registered
   since the removal (05:30:49 UTC on 4 October) with no row written for it,
   so the check was evidence rather than vacuous. Afterwards, through the
   public API, the table returns 404 "could not find the table", while
   `reviews` returns 401 "permission denied". The same request tells missing
   from forbidden. Supabase's own backups may hold the rows until they age
   out; the policy says "deleted from our database".

### Signing in returns you where you came from (4 October 2026)
Signing in from `/admin` landed on `/course` (`cf1d733`). There were two
causes:
- The admin "Sign in" link stored no return path.
- Two pieces of code navigate after sign-in, and they raced. On the local
  stub, the one that sends signed-in users on `/auth` to `/course` won every
  time, so the existing lesson return-to didn't land either.

Both now ask one function for the destination. Only lesson paths and exactly
`/admin` are accepted. Tested locally:
- lesson return-to: lands on the lesson (against the unfixed code, `/course`);
- admin return-to: lands on `/admin`;
- a forged stored value: falls back to `/course`;
- an already signed-in visit to `/auth`: goes to `/course`.

**Observed on production by the owner on 4 October: signing in from
`/admin` returned straight to `/admin`.**

### Privacy and erasure
- The privacy policy and the database disagreed about what happens to a
  certificate when an account is deleted. The policy now matches the database
  (`6135b4f`): erasure deletes the certificate, and its verification link
  stops working. **ERASURE-PROCEDURE.md** exists. No one
  has asked yet, so it has never run end to end. Read it before the first
  time.
- **Housekeeping, 3 October:** stale branches deleted
  (`migration-012-stats-signature`, the unmerged Vercel Web Analytics branch,
  which would have added a third-party tracker, and `tmp-3c`, whose commits
  were all already in `main`); the throwaway flag-on preview deployment
  removed. Only `main` and `audit-fixes` remain.
- **Standing rule:** preview deployments never get production database
  credentials (SECURITY-NOTES.md). Previews can't test database behaviour.
  That's the price, and it's deliberate.

## 3. What can't be known

- **Whether a stale browser tab still calls `/api/track`.** Vercel doesn't log
  requests to a route that no longer exists, so the logs can't say. The
  shipped JavaScript can, and it contains no call. A stale tab would get a
  silent 404 and write nothing.
- **How long Supabase's backups keep the dropped rows.** The plan's backup
  retention wasn't measured. The policy wording was chosen to be true
  regardless: "deleted from our database".
- **Which accounts belong to the pre-Supabase 22-lesson cohort.** Migration
  stamped them like everyone else and left no marker. Claims are handled by
  hand (CERTIFICATE-CLAIMS.md), and a key fingerprint that matched 12
  accounts was rejected because at least 7 of them were current learners.

## 4. How things were checked, and where checks failed

Four lessons, all in VERIFICATION-NOTES.md with the cases:

- **A zero proves nothing until a control finds something.** Brand searches
  gave false cleans twice (case; `·` minified to `\xb7`). A log query with a
  leading slash matched nothing even when the request was there. The
  `/api/track` bundle check meant something only once it found the call on
  pre-deploy production.
- **Verify the behaviour, not the declaration.** A migration that changed
  nothing, a setup file declaring an index production never had, a
  `vercel.json` redirect that never fired, a grandfather clause matching no
  account.
- **The buffer is what runs.** Supabase's "new query" address once landed on
  a saved snippet holding `009_revoke_all_sessions.sql`. The empty-buffer
  check and the exact-match check before Run both refused it.
- **Some instruments can't see the thing at all.** Requests to a removed
  route never reach Vercel's logs.

Mistakes of my own, for the record:
- A shell heredoc ate backslashes and corrupted a guard file once; a later
  one ate the code spans in a SECURITY-NOTES section. Both were caught by
  reading the file back before commit.
- The first draft of the 009 note said the editor "loaded blank". What was
  observed was no buffer at 10 s and the snippet at 16 s. Corrected
  (`9f715b1`).
- The timeout commit `6b8bdb5` reached `main` before its preview check,
  because a URL lookup came back empty and the command carried on. The
  production build passed and the change had been verified locally, but it
  wasn't the agreed order.
- Item 2 of this report's open items went through a shell command that
  stripped two code spans, and it was merged before being read back
  (`a9cdad7`, fixed in `3779f6e`). The standing rule since then: no markdown
  through a shell command, and every file read back before merging.

## 5. Open items, ranked

1. **ERASURE-PROCEDURE.md step 5 should load the homepage twice.** It checks
   that an erased person's name is gone from the live homepage after a
   rebuild. On 4 October the first request after a rebuild still returned the
   old page (see "First reviews published"). So a single load can show the
   old page, name included, even when the rebuild worked. The step should
   load the page, wait a few seconds, load it again, and check the second
   load.
2. **Signups after the `/api/track` removal: checked, normal rate.** In the
   16½ hours from 15:31 UTC on 3 October to 08:10 UTC on 4 October, which
   includes the whole Lagos evening, 1 account was created. In the 24 hours
   before, 18 were. A read-only per-day count of `auth.users` for the 42
   days to 4 October (208 accounts) puts the normal rate at about 3 a day
   (median 3, mean 3.1 over 24 August to 26 September), with bursts on 27 and
   28 September (38, 29) and 3 October (15). The 18 were mostly that 3 October
   burst, which peaked at 09:00 UTC with 6 in the hour. The last signup before
   the removal was at 13:33:43 UTC, two hours before the deploy, so the quiet
   began before the change, not at it. At 3 a day, about 2 signups would be
   expected in 16½ hours, so 1 is unremarkable. Nothing to do unless the daily
   count stays at 0 for several days. If it does, register a test account and
   delete it with ERASURE-PROCEDURE.md. What causes the bursts (X threads,
   say) was never measured; treat that as a hypothesis.
3. **Next.js 14 → 16.** Two high advisories remain in shipped dependencies;
   SECURITY-NOTES explains why neither is live. Two majors: re-verify that
   public lessons are in the server HTML and gated ones aren't.
4. **Regenerate `SUPABASE_SETUP.sql` from the live database** (`pg_dump
   --schema-only`). It has been patched by hand repeatedly (reviews appended,
   `course_events` removed), and it has been wrong before.
5. **The local production build fails on Windows** at `/opengraph-image`
   ("Invalid URL" inside `@vercel/og`). It predates this work, and Vercel
   builds fine, but a local build can't serve as a pre-push check.
6. **Supabase saved snippets**: about 30 "Untitled query" entries, including
   one-off migrations that must never run twice. The owner is deleting the
   009 one; the rest should go once their migrations are in
   `supabase/migrations/`.
7. **The signed-in app pages are client-rendered and noindex.** That is the
   steady state by design. Rendering them on the server is optional SEO work,
   not a defect.
